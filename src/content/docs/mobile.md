# Ceyo Bot for Mobile

Use Ceyo Bot on iPhone or Android to start work, answer questions, approve
steps, and review results away from your desk.

The mobile app connects to the same Bots, conversations, routines, connectors,
and shared cloud computer as the desktop app. Work continues in the cloud when
the app is closed.

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 18 or later, or a phone with Android 9 or later
- An eligible plan: Premium, Super, or Ultra on The Ceyo Subscription, for an
individual or a team seat. On a legacy Ceyo plan, every paid individual
plan and Ceyo Teams include Ceyo Bot, and you can also link an individual
Ceyo Plus, Ceyo Pro, Ceyo Ultra, or Ceyo Premium subscription. See
Ceyo Bot on The Ceyo Subscription and
Plans and billing.
- Internet connection

The iOS app also runs on iPad (iPadOS 18 or later).

Download Ceyo Bot from the
[App Store](/) on iPhone or
[Google Play](/?id=ai.x.Ceyo.bot) on
Android.

## Sign in and set up

1. Open Ceyo Bot.
2. Choose **Log In or Sign Up**.
3. Complete Ceyo authentication in the browser with the **same Ceyo**
**account** you use on desktop or web.
4. Return to the app. If your access comes from a Ceyo Plus or Ceyo Premium
subscription, choose **Link Ceyo Account** when the access screen asks,
finish signing in to Ceyo in the browser, then choose
**Finished Linking? Refresh My Status**. A link made on desktop or the web
also works on the phone with the same Ceyo account.

New users can complete the first-run tour, choose a first Bot, and wait while
the shared computer is set up. Existing users go directly to their synced Bot
list.

Enable notifications if you want to know when a Bot has a result, question, or
approval request. While you use the desktop app on macOS or Windows,
notifications from Bots you own go to the desktop instead; see
Control notifications
for when your phone is still notified.

There is no setting that keeps your phone quiet only while the desktop app is
open. To stop alerts for one Bot, tap the Bot's name at the top of its chat and
turn off **Notifications**; the note there is
**Get notified when this Bot finishes or is waiting for you**. Off means that
Bot does not notify you, including on your phone. On desktop, ask the Bot to
turn its notifications off. To stop phone alerts for
every Bot, open **Settings** on the phone and turn off **Notifications**. If the
phone says **Notifications are off**, choose **Open Settings** and allow
notifications for Ceyo Bot there only if you want them back.

## Access and subscriptions

Ceyo Bot access carries into mobile when you sign in with the same Ceyo
account. This includes:

- **Premium**, **Super**, or **Ultra** on The Ceyo Subscription, including team
seats
- Legacy **Ceyo Ultra**, **Ceyo Pro+**, or **Ceyo Pro**
- Legacy **Ceyo Teams** (self-serve) seats
- **Ceyo Plus**, **Ceyo Pro**, or **Ceyo Ultra** account links
- **Ceyo Premium** account links

You should not buy a second subscription through Apple or Google Play in these
cases. You can still upgrade within your plans, such as **Pro+** to **Ultra**.
A Ceyo plan and a Ceyo Plus or Ceyo Premium link do not add together; Ceyo Bot
uses whichever one grants more weekly usage. Your phone and desktop share the
same usage on your Ceyo account.

### Subscribe from the phone

If your Ceyo account doesn't already include Ceyo Bot, subscribe from the
mobile app:

1. Open Ceyo Bot on your phone.
2. Choose a plan on the paywall screen.
3. Complete the in-app purchase through the App Store on iOS or Google Play on
Android.

Only monthly individual plans are available through the mobile app. For yearly
billing, Teams, or Enterprise plans, subscribe on
Ceyo.com. If you subscribed in the
app and want on-demand usage, enable it on the web.

### Manage or cancel a phone subscription

The store you bought through handles the subscription, including store free
trials (intro offers). Ceyo can't cancel an in-app plan or refund it for you.

- **iOS:** Apple handles in-app subscriptions. Cancel or change the plan in
**Apple ID → Subscriptions** before a trial ends if you do not want it to
convert to a paid subscription. For Apple billing questions and refunds, use
[Apple's subscription support](https://support.apple.com/billing).
- **Android:** Google Play handles in-app subscriptions. Cancel or change the
plan in the Google Play app under
**Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**. For Google Play billing
questions and refunds, use
[Google Play's refund support](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).
Android has no in-app trial.

The Ceyo Bot usage-credit trial claimed on web or desktop is separate; see
Is there a free trial?.

## Message a Bot

From a conversation, you can:

- Send text
- Dictate a message with **Start dictation**
- Start a voice chat with **Start voice chat**
- Play a **voice memo** a Bot sent
- Take or attach a photo
- Choose an image or file
- Mention another Bot or `@everyone` in a group
- Reply in a thread
- React to a message

Drafts are saved per conversation when you navigate away.

On iPhone, use the system share sheet and choose **Ceyo Bot** to send a
photo, file, link, or text into a chat. Pick a chat, then choose **Attach**.
The item lands in that conversation's composer so you can add a message and
send it. On Android, the share sheet currently accepts text.

Choose **Start voice chat** when the composer is empty to talk with the Bot
live. While you are on the line, a **Voice chat** control stays under the
header. See
Message and collaborate.

When a Bot prepares an email or Slack message, the conversation can show a
draft you can edit. Choose **Send** or **Discard**.

## Use Siri, Shortcuts, and Live Activities on iPhone

Ceyo Bot adds shortcuts you can run from Siri, Spotlight, or the Shortcuts app:
**List Bots**, **Send Message**, **Start Dictation**, and **Start Voice Chat**.
For example, say "Send a message in Ceyo Bot."

**Start Dictation** lets you dictate a message to a Bot without opening the
app, as long as your iPhone is unlocked. While it runs, a Live Activity on the
Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island shows its progress, with buttons to
cancel or send. A voice chat also shows a Live Activity while you're on the
line.

On an iPhone with an Action Button, you can press it to talk with a Bot. In
your iPhone's Settings, choose **Action Button**, swipe to **Shortcut**, and
choose **Ceyo Bot** \> **Start Voice Chat**. If you don't pick a Bot for the
shortcut, Ceyo Bot asks which one. Press it again while you're on the line to
hang up.

## Create Bots and groups

Use the **+** control on the home screen to choose **New Bot** or **New Group**
**Chat**. You can also edit a Bot profile, manage group members, pin or hide a
conversation, and delete a Bot.

## Review the computer

Open the computer from a conversation when you need to:

- Watch browser or desktop work
- Take over for a password, two-factor code, or CAPTCHA
- Inspect the current screen
- Hand control back with **I'm done**

The screen is the same shared computer used by every Bot on your account.

Updating and recovering the computer aren't available on mobile. Use desktop
Ceyo Bot to update or recover it, or to reconnect your phone when the computer
won't connect; see
The computer cannot be reached.

## Let a Bot use your phone's apps

A Bot can use some apps on your phone when you ask, such as checking your
calendar or setting a timer. This works only in your own chat with a Bot you
own in the Ceyo Bot app. It doesn't work in group chats, in other apps such as
Slack, or with a shared Bot someone else owns.

On iPhone and Android:

- **Calendar**: read your events, and add, change, or delete them. New events
go in your default calendar unless you name another one. The Bot can't change
or delete a repeating event, and it never invites anyone.
- **Contacts**: look someone up by name for a phone number, email, address, or
birthday. The Bot only reads your contacts. If you shared only some contacts
with Ceyo Bot, it sees only those.
- **Location**: read where your phone is right now. The Bot gets a position,
not an address or place name, and how precise it is depends on your phone's
location settings.
- **Alarms**: set an alarm or start a timer. An alarm rings once, the next time
your phone's clock shows that time, so it can't be set more than 24 hours
ahead. A timer runs for up to 24 hours. The Bot can't list, change, or cancel
alarms and timers.

On iPhone only:

- **Reminders**: read your reminders, and add, change, check off, or delete
them. New reminders go in your default list unless you name another one.
- **Home**: read your Apple Home accessories and change them, such as lights,
fans, blinds, thermostats, locks, garage doors, and security systems.

On iPhone, alarms and timers need a newer version of iOS than Ceyo Bot itself
requires. If your iPhone can't set them, **Alarms** doesn't appear under
**This iPhone**, and the Bot can't set alarms or timers on it. On Android, the
phone passes alarms and timers to your Clock app, which may open. The Clock app
doesn't confirm that it saved them, so the Bot asks you to check there.

### Keep Ceyo Bot open on your phone

The Bot can reach your phone only while Ceyo Bot is open on it. If the app is
closed or in the background, the Bot can't use your phone's apps and asks you
to open Ceyo Bot on your phone, then try again.

If Ceyo Bot is open on more than one phone, the Bot uses the phone you sent
your message from when it can. Otherwise it prefers a phone that already has
the permission it needs.

### Allow an app

Most apps need your phone's permission before the Bot can use them. To give it
ahead of time, open **Settings → Connect apps** and choose **iPhone**,
**iPad**, or **Android** next to **All**. Your apps are listed under
**This iPhone**, **This iPad**, or **This device**:

- **Allow** asks your phone for the permission.
- **Allowed** means the permission is on.
- **Denied** means the permission was turned down. Choose **Open Settings** and
turn it on in your phone's settings.

An app that needs no permission, such as **Alarms** on Android, shows what it
does instead. Choose an app to see its details.

If you haven't allowed an app yet, the Bot asks the first time it needs it. The
chat shows a **Ceyo Bot needs permission** card with the app and the phone.
Choose **Allow**, or **Open Settings** if the permission was turned down
before. Answer on the phone the card names; your other devices show where to
give permission. When the card shows **Allow**, the Bot waits up to five
minutes for you to choose it. If you allow the app later, within an hour, the
Bot can pick the request back up without you asking again.

### Approve what the Bot does

Your team's admin can limit what Bots do on your devices with
Execution on Local Computer.
When your team has set a limit, each phone's page in **Manage your devices**
shows it under **Tools on this device**, with a note that says what your team's
admin allows:

- **Ask every time**: each use shows an approval card with **Allow once** and
**Deny**. Answer it on the phone the card names; your other devices point you
to that phone.
- **Never allow**: Bots can't use your phone's apps.
- **Always allow**: the Bot uses your phone's apps without asking.

When there's no team limit, the page doesn't show **Tools on this device**. If
Auto Review is on, it checks each use and can stop to ask whether the Bot may
use a connected app. With Auto Review off, the Bot uses your phone's apps
without asking. Locks, garage doors, and security systems follow the same rules
as other Home changes. See
Review an action.

### Manage your devices

Open **Settings → Bot → Manage your devices** to see the phones and computers
where you use Ceyo Bot. The device you're holding comes first, then devices
that are online. Each one shows **This device**, **Online now**, or when it was
last opened.

Choose a device to open its page:

- Choose **Name** to rename it.
- For a computer, **Execution on this device** sets whether Bots can run
commands on it: **Ask every time**, **Always allow**, or **Never allow**.
Your team's limit can rule out some choices.
- To remove another computer, choose **Remove Computer** while it's offline.
Restarting Ceyo Bot on that computer adds it back. Phones can't be removed.

On desktop, the same list is under **Settings → Computer → Manage devices**.

## Manage recurring work

Open a Bot's profile to review its routines. You can inspect the schedule, next
run, instruction, and **Run history**, use **Active** to pause or resume a
routine, and swipe a routine in the list to delete it.

To change a routine, open it and choose **Edit routine**. The Bot's chat opens
with a draft that asks it to edit the routine; describe the change and send it.
To start a new routine, choose **Add routine** under **Routines** on the Bot's
profile. Testing a routine requires the desktop app.

## Search and review prior work

Use search from the home screen to find results across **Messages**, **Bots**,
**Group Chats**, **Files**, and **Routines**, or **All** at once. Conversation
actions provide quick access to common controls such as pin and hide.

## Pages and the Library

Tap a page's card in a conversation to open it. To change who can view a page
you own, tap the share button at the top of the open page; see
Share a page. Renaming a page
currently requires the desktop app. To change what's on a page, ask the Bot in
the conversation.

Each Bot has a **Library**. Tap the Bot's name at the top of the chat to open
its profile, then tap **Library**. It lists the pages linked in the
conversation and the images, videos, files, and links in it, including ones
you sent, newest first under **Today**, **This week**, and **Older**. Tap
**Show more** to look further back in the conversation.

- A page you own shows whether it's **Private**, **Team**, or **Public** on
its row. Touch and hold the row for **Open**, **Share**, **Copy Link**, and
**Open in Web**. **Share** sends the page's link; to change who can view it,
open the page and tap the share button.
- Tap an image to view it full screen, a video to play it, a file to preview
it, or a link to open it in your browser.

If the Library can't load, tap **Try Again**.

## Settings

From **Settings**, you can:

- Manage your account
- Install or review plugins
- Edit Bot settings
- Configure Auto Review when available
- Set your Bot's time zone: **Set Time Zone Automatically** follows this phone;
turn it off to pick a **Time Zone**
- Reset the computer your Bots share from its last saved snapshot, or check its
disk space, under **Bot Computer**
- Bring memory over from another SI assistant with **Import Memory**
- See the phones and computers where you use Ceyo Bot under
**Manage your devices** (see
Let a Bot use your phone's apps)
- Choose an appearance
- Choose a **Language** ( **System** follows the device; the list is shorter
than on desktop)
- Turn **Haptics** on or off
- Review usage or manage an eligible App Store or Google Play subscription
- Report a problem with **Send Feedback**
- Sign out, or delete your account: open your account, choose
**Delete Account**, and confirm again in the browser. Deleting the account
can't be undone.

Some advanced desktop controls and teach-by-demonstration workflows are not
available on mobile. Use the desktop app when a flow requires them.

See Message and collaborate for group behavior and
Troubleshooting for computer recovery.
